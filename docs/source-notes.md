# Source Notes

These notes summarise the source trail used to build the draft Quandamooka Film Festival toolkit.

## Source posture

The local archive documents were treated as old brainstorm material and background notes only. They were not treated as community approval, legal advice, cultural authority, partner confirmation, or a final festival rulebook.

The public site does not present QYAC, MMEICAC, Elders, Council, schools, sponsors, businesses, or community groups as partners, supporters, judges, hosts, or endorsers.
