An optional AI film assistant, not a replacement director.

AI can make the planning load lighter. The creator, team, participants, and reviewers still decide what belongs in the story.

Human-Led Workflow

Where AI can help, step by step.

Ideation

Generate options, loglines, questions, and directions without choosing the meaning for the creator.

Cultural context

Gather source notes and questions to ask. Do not treat AI output as cultural approval.

Character and place

Help organise details, risk notes, consent notes, and what should be checked with people involved.

Plot and structure

Offer scene orders, turning points, pacing options, and gaps the human may want to fill.

Script

Draft beats, captions, interview question paths, voiceover options, or dialogue notes for review.

Visuals

Create rough prompt drafts for mood, camera, framing, and storyboard tests.

Casting feasibility

Help simplify scenes, props, locations, and schedules so the film can be made with real resources.

Revisions

Compare versions, list unresolved questions, and keep source notes visible.

Animatic or pre-viz

Use temporary images, rough timing, and placeholder sound to test the plan before filming.

Production pack

Prepare shot lists, release-form needs, gear lists, file plans, captions, and edit notes.

Filming support

Keep checklists handy. People on the ground make the calls.

Post-production

Support captions, summaries, edit logs, version notes, and source trails.

Final review

Check consent, music, footage, cultural care, privacy, credits, and public/private boundaries before sharing.

Good Handoff

Tell AI what it can and cannot do.

Helpful instruction

Help me organise this story plan. Ask questions where I have gaps. Do not invent permissions, cultural authority, quotes, private details, or facts I have not supplied.

Useful output

Return a Markdown outline with open questions, source links, permission checks, possible shot list, and one next action.
