Screening ideas that stay clearly draft.

Outdoor cinema, film club, workshops, and small community programs can be explored without pretending a full festival structure already exists.

Programming Ideas

Possible formats to test.

Outdoor cinema

A relaxed night screening when rights, permissions, weather, sound, access, and site care are sorted.

Film club

A recurring small-group format for watching, discussing, and learning from films with source notes.

Community screening

A local session built around consent, introductions, discussion, and clear boundaries.

Youth workshop

Simple phone filmmaking, interview planning, captions, and review support for young creators.

Stories from the Sand

A draft theme connecting beach, sport, place, movement, memory, and short films.

AI storyboard table

A practical station where people test rough scene prompts while keeping cultural and privacy boundaries visible.

Adjacent Projects

Related, not the QFF home.

Sandy Sports, Ballow Road, and Amity Point are adjacent idea shelves. They can help with place, screening, sport, and community context, but they should not be presented as festival hosts, partners, or approvals.
