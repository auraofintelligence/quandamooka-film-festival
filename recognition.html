What would need review first?

  • Who is allowed to nominate and who is allowed to decide?
  • How are youth, consent, privacy, and cultural material protected?
  • How does the festival avoid rewarding unapproved or extractive storytelling?
  • What language keeps recognition encouraging without turning it into a gatekeeping rulebook?
