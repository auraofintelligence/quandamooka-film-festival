Submission readiness without the gatekeeping vibe.

This is a friendly check before a film is shared, screened, or sent anywhere. It helps people protect the story and the people in it.

Checklist

Ask these before sharing.

  • Do you own or have permission for the music, images, voices, and footage?
  • Have people in the film agreed to be included?
  • Is any cultural material handled with care and review?
  • Is your film link easy to open?
  • Do you know what should stay private?
  • What help do you need next?

Friendly Rule Of Thumb

If a detail could harm, expose, misrepresent, or pressure someone, pause.

Pausing is not failure. It is part of respectful storytelling. A film can be stronger because it waited for the right permission, review, edit, or private boundary.
